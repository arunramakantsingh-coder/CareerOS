# CareerOS — Product Vision & Scope

## Product Definition

CareerOS is a web-based, multi-tenant AI-powered Global Career Operating System for:

- job seekers
- international career movers
- executives

It combines career intelligence, semantic job discovery, personalized application generation, career CRM, global mobility intelligence, interview intelligence and long-term career decision support.

## Immediate Product

**v0.1 — Personal Job & Interview Copilot**

It must be genuinely useful to the first real user, not a demonstration application.

## Strategic Evolution

```text
v0.1 Personal Job & Interview Copilot
        ↓
v0.2 Global Job Intelligence
        ↓
v0.3 Global Mobility
        ↓
v1/v2 SaaS
```

## Product Differentiator

Search, ranking and matching must understand:

- capabilities
- responsibilities
- technologies
- architecture domains
- leadership
- governance
- industry
- seniority
- transferable skills

Title similarity must not dominate.

## Seven Major Engines

1. Career Intelligence Engine
2. Persona Engine
3. Job Intelligence Engine
4. Matching Engine
5. Application Factory
6. Global Mobility Engine
7. Career CRM & Outcome Engine

## Cross-cutting intelligence

- Truth & Compliance
- Skill Gap Intelligence
- Remote Intelligence
- Company Intelligence
- Interview Intelligence
- AI Orchestration
- Analytics
- Security/Governance

## North Star

Continuously understand the user's professional identity and connect it to the best realistic opportunities worldwide: jobs, remote work, relocation and migration, while keeping the user in control of important decisions and submissions.

## SaaS

Indicative future tiers:

- Free
- Pro
- Global
- Executive

Architecture must support subscriptions from the beginning; payment integration may be deferred.

## Product success metrics

- relevant-job rate
- application-to-interview conversion
- interview-to-offer conversion
- time saved per application
- truth-validation pass rate
- job-source coverage
- duplicate suppression
- retention
- persona usage
- international pathway engagement
- paid conversion later

## Key product principles

1. Career-centric, not title-centric.
2. Career Vault/Career Passport is authoritative.
3. Evidence is the basis of claims.
4. Job DNA is the canonical structured opportunity representation.
5. Hard failures are explicit.
6. Matching is explainable.
7. Skill gaps are persistent career-development data.
8. Global mobility is a connected but distinct decision layer.
9. Human approval is required for sensitive/irreversible actions.
10. Future capabilities may be visible in the UI only as clearly marked planned functionality.
