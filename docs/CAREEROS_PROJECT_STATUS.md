## Immediate User Objective

Deliver a working personal system for:
- global/remote job discovery
- India WFH/contract opportunities
- location-specific office opportunities
- semantic JD matching
- application tailoring
- company intelligence
- interview preparation
- live interview assistance

## Critical Product Rule

Search broadly using title plus JD/capability understanding.

Apply using the employer's advertised title.

Persona titles are internal matching/positioning constructs.
