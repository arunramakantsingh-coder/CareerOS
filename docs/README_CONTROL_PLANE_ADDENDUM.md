### Current next milestone

`M02 — Identity + Career Intake`

The target journey begins at the public CareerOS website, proceeds through sign-up/sign-in, then moves immediately into CV and professional-document intake. Authentication, identity linking, document vault, AI extraction foundation and profile completeness are treated as one coherent onboarding flow rather than implementing a login page in isolation.
